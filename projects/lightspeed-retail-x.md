# Project 1: Lightspeed Retail X Point-of-Sale Implementation

**Organization:** Condor Latino Services  
**My role:** Retail management and POS implementation  
**Technology:** Lightspeed Retail X (formerly Vend), point-of-sale systems, inventory processes

## The challenge

Putting a new point-of-sale system into everyday store operations takes more than installing software. Product information and transaction processes need to be set up in ways employees can follow during busy shifts.

## What I did

- **Set up and implemented** Lightspeed Retail X for daily retail use.
- **Supported** the product, inventory, and sales transaction workflows involved in the transition.
- **Trained** 12 employees on how to use the system in their regular work.
- **Helped** staff adjust to the new process while maintaining customer service.

## Results

The store implemented Lightspeed Retail X for its retail workflows, and **12 employees received training**. I did not collect reliable before-and-after performance percentages, so I am not claiming a specific time or cost saving.

## What I learned

Good technical implementation depends on communication. It matters whether the people using a system understand how it fits into the job they do each day.
